# The rank-kernel taxonomy closure — Bonferroni harmonic, Mehran linear, Gini delta=2, S-Gini delta=3 as the four-class structural treatment that closes the rank-weighted Lorenz-area family on the pew-insights inequality stack as of v0.6.291

## 0. The problem axis-43 through axis-47 implicitly opens

Across roughly two days of focused inequality-stack landings, the pew-insights repository shipped four cross-source axes whose mathematical structure is "rank-weighted area-under-Lorenz-like" rather than "concave-share-power" or "log-deviation". In landing order: Bonferroni at axis-43 (v0.6.285), Mehran with de-Vergottini cross-anchor at axis-45 (v0.6.289, sha bc7380c), and the S-Gini delta=3 extension at axis-47 (v0.6.291, sha 665e13f). Bracketed in the middle are axis-44 (Kolm-Pollak, absolute-invariance class) and axis-46 (Wolfson polarization), which are in different families entirely. So the rank-kernel family on disk is now four axes — Gini already at axis-21 since long before this sprint, plus the three new ones.

Each of those four axes is, strictly, a SCALAR functional of the per-day `total_tokens` distribution. But each ALSO sits inside a continuous parametric family, and the choice of which parameter to fix and ship as a separate axis is itself a structural decision. This post argues the four landed axes are not four arbitrary parameter pins on one continuum — they are, structurally, the FOUR-CLASS PARTITION of the rank-kernel family by kernel functional form: HARMONIC (Bonferroni), LINEAR (Mehran), QUADRATIC (Gini, equivalently S-Gini delta=2), and HIGHER-POLYNOMIAL (S-Gini delta=3 and beyond). That partition closes the family in the sense that any further rank-kernel axis would either repeat one of these four classes at a different parameter value, or live entirely outside the family. The post walks the partition, derives why each axis sits where it does, surfaces the cross-axis identity bridges between adjacent classes, and closes with the falsifiability statement: under what corpus shape would the four-class partition collapse and read as a single redundant family.

## 1. The rank-kernel family written abstractly

Sort the per-day total_tokens for one source as `x_(1) <= x_(2) <= ... <= x_(n)` with rank `i` running from 1 (smallest) to n (largest), and let `mu = (1/n) sum x_i`. A RANK-KERNEL inequality functional has the general form

    I_K = (1 / mu) * sum_{i=1..n} K(i, n) * (mu - x_(i))

where `K(i, n)` is a non-negative weight depending only on rank position `i` and sample size `n`, normalized so that on the equality vector (every `x_i = mu`) we get `I_K = 0`. Different choices of `K` give different members of the family. The Pigou-Dalton transfer principle is satisfied iff `K(i, n)` is non-increasing in `i` (i.e. the rank kernel down-weights higher ranks), which is the structural condition that distinguishes rank-kernel inequality functionals from rank-kernel POLARIZATION functionals (which use signed kernels — e.g. Wolfson at axis-46).

The four landed axes correspond to four families of `K`:

- **Bonferroni (axis-43, v0.6.285)**: `K(i, n) = (1/n) * sum_{j=i..n} 1/j`. The kernel is HARMONIC — falls off as the running tail sum of `1/j`. This puts disproportionate weight on the BOTTOM ranks because the tail sum is largest at `i = 1`.
- **Mehran (axis-45, v0.6.289)**: `K(i, n) = (2/n) * (1 - i/(n+1))`. The kernel is LINEAR in rank — falls off as a straight line from rank 1 to rank n. Compared to Bonferroni, the bottom-rank emphasis is muted; compared to Gini, the top-rank emphasis is muted.
- **Gini / S-Gini delta=2 (axis-21)**: `K(i, n)` proportional to `(2(n - i) + 1)`, equivalently the Gini classical rank kernel; in the S-Gini parameterization (Donaldson-Weymark) this is the delta=2 case, kernel `delta * (delta - 1) * (1 - F(x))^(delta - 2)` evaluated at the empirical CDF. The kernel is QUADRATIC in the share-rank sense (the area-under-Lorenz interpretation gives the quadratic moment).
- **S-Gini delta=3 (axis-47, v0.6.291)**: `K(i, n)` follows the delta=3 case of the same Donaldson-Weymark family, which is CUBIC in `(1 - F(x))`. Higher `delta` makes the kernel even more bottom-tail-emphasizing in a different way than Bonferroni — Bonferroni's harmonic falloff is heaviest at the very bottom (`i = 1`) and decays fast; S-Gini delta=3's cubic falloff is heaviest in the BOTTOM HALF and decays smoothly.

The four kernels — harmonic, linear, quadratic, cubic — are functional-form-distinct and not mutual reparameterizations. That is the four-class partition claim.

## 2. Why each landed at its parameter pin

**Bonferroni at axis-43.** The Bonferroni 1930 functional is parameter-free — there is no continuous knob to sweep. The harmonic kernel comes from cumulating the rank reciprocals `1/j`, and any modification breaks the cumulative-Lorenz interpretation that defines the index. So axis-43 lands as a SCALAR, not a parametric family. The CHANGELOG live-smoke at v0.6.285 reads `claude-code 0.8594 vs opencode 0.3441`, a 2.50x rank-amplified spread (see axis-43 walk-through for the full read). The relevant cross-axis identity is `Bonferroni / Gini` — the Bonferroni-over-Gini ratio is a witness for bottom-tail concentration, and on the corpus this ratio is largest on opencode (smallest distribution = most uniform = ratio closest to 1) and smallest on the heaviest-tailed sources. The cross-anchor that ships is `bonferroniOverGini` and the flag is `--include-gini-anchor`.

**Mehran at axis-45.** Mehran 1976 is also parameter-free in its scalar form. The linear-in-rank kernel `(2/n) * (1 - i/(n+1))` is what makes Mehran the cleanest "single-row tail-dominance diagnostic" the inequality stack carries — it does not over-weight the bottom (Bonferroni does that) and does not over-weight the top (de-Vergottini does that). The de-Vergottini cross-anchor that ships at v0.6.289 (sha bc7380c, see axis-45 walk-through) is the TOP-tail dual of Mehran: same linear kernel form but oriented from the top rank down instead of the bottom rank up. The bottom-vs-top spectrum is therefore: Bonferroni (bottom-tail heavy, harmonic) -> Mehran (linear, balanced) -> de-Vergottini (top-tail heavy, linear inverted). That is a three-point structural treatment of the linear-kernel sub-family. The equality-identity witness shipping at bc7380c is the numerical-stability floor for this three-point structure.

**Gini at axis-21.** Gini is the historical anchor — every other rank-kernel axis on disk is benchmarked against it via a `--include-gini-anchor` cross-anchor flag. Structurally, Gini is the S-Gini delta=2 case, and the QUADRATIC kernel is the unique kernel within the S-Gini family that satisfies the Lorenz-area interpretation (twice the area between the diagonal and the Lorenz curve). The choice to ship Gini as a separate axis rather than as an alias for `--delta 2` of a unified S-Gini axis was a historical-priority decision, not a structural one — both surfaces ship today and the cross-anchor identity `S-Gini(delta=2) - Gini` is at numerical-stability floor (≤ 1e-15) on every source.

**S-Gini delta=3 at axis-47.** The Donaldson-Weymark 1980 generalization opens the delta knob and asks: what does the family look like at higher delta? The axis-47 walk-through (see post `2026-05-01-axis-47-daily-token-s-gini-delta-3-walkthrough-pew-insights-v0-6-291-sha-665e13f`) lands delta=3 specifically because (a) it is the smallest integer delta strictly above the Gini case, (b) the cubic-in-(1-F) kernel is structurally distinct from the quadratic Gini kernel rather than a smooth perturbation, and (c) the property-based suite at sha 665e13f instruments three structural invariants (delta-monotonicity in the rank kernel, the infinity-limit collapse to the maximin, and the builder cross-anchor identity to Gini at delta=2) that pin the entire delta-sweep, not just the delta=3 point. So axis-47 is the parametric-family axis of the four — Bonferroni and Mehran are scalar, Gini is the historical scalar anchor, and S-Gini delta=3 is the parametric handle.

The four-class partition is therefore: harmonic/scalar (axis-43), linear/scalar with top-tail dual (axis-45), quadratic/scalar=parametric anchor (axis-21=S-Gini delta=2), polynomial/parametric (axis-47). Closed.

## 3. Cross-axis identities that bridge the four classes

The bridges that ship as cross-anchor flags on the four axes are not ornamental — they encode the structural relationships between the kernel forms.

**Bonferroni-Gini bridge.** Both kernels are bottom-tail-emphasizing relative to their average rank weight, but Bonferroni decays harmonically (faster initial drop, slower long tail) and Gini decays linearly. The ratio `B / G` is therefore a SHAPE indicator for the bottom-tail decay rate of the actual distribution — when the corpus distribution itself has a heavy bottom tail relative to the middle, B/G is large; when the bottom tail is thin, B/G approaches the equality limit (which is well-defined and finite). The cross-anchor flag on axis-43 is `--include-gini-anchor` and the live-smoke at v0.6.285 reports the ratio explicitly per source.

**Mehran-Gini bridge.** Both kernels are smooth and monotone, but Mehran's linear kernel and Gini's quadratic kernel differ in how they accumulate rank weight. The ratio `M / G` is a measure of how much the inequality reading depends on the SECOND-MOMENT of the rank weighting versus the first moment. On a uniform distribution the ratio is exactly known in closed form (= 3/4 in the continuous limit); on the corpus, deviations from that limit signal corpus-shape effects orthogonal to the headline inequality magnitude.

**Mehran-de-Vergottini bridge.** This is the cleanest top-vs-bottom diagnostic the stack carries. Both axes use the linear kernel, but Mehran orients it from the bottom and de-Vergottini orients it from the top. The DIFFERENCE `M - dV` is sign-symmetric on a perfectly symmetric distribution and signed on any asymmetric distribution. The axis-45 walk-through documents the live-smoke values; the bridge is the falsifiability handle for "this corpus has a heavier bottom tail than top tail" claims.

**Gini-S-Gini delta-sweep bridge.** The S-Gini family at delta=2 collapses identically to Gini, providing the equality-identity witness at numerical-stability floor. As `delta` increases, the index reads strictly larger on any non-equal distribution (delta-monotonicity invariant, instrumented at sha 665e13f); as `delta -> infinity`, the index approaches the MAXIMIN deficit (ratio of minimum to mean), the infinity-limit invariant. So the delta-sweep traces a continuous interpolation between the Gini-quadratic anchor and the maximin-extremal endpoint, and the choice of delta=3 as the shipped pin is the cleanest single point on that curve where the cubic kernel is structurally distinct from quadratic but not yet asymptotically dominated by the maximin endpoint.

These four bridges are the cross-axis identity scaffolding of the rank-kernel family. Removing any one of them collapses the four-class partition.

## 4. Where the four-class partition could collapse

The partition is a STRUCTURAL claim about kernel functional forms, not an EMPIRICAL claim about the corpus values. It would collapse in two ways:

**Collapse A: corpus shape forces the four readings to agree.** On a perfectly equal corpus (every day same token count), all four axes read 0 simultaneously; the headline numbers are degenerate and the partition is invisible. On a perfectly Pareto-distributed corpus with the right shape parameter, the four kernel forms can give numerically close readings even though the kernels are structurally distinct. The corpus today is neither — the live-smoke spreads on Bonferroni (0.34 to 0.86), Gini, Mehran, and S-Gini delta=3 are wide and the rank orderings of the six sources differ between axes. So the partition is empirically witnessed today.

**Collapse B: the rank-kernel family is shown to be a re-parameterization of the concave-share-power family.** The Atkinson-Chakravarty closed-form identity `A = C(2-C)` at alpha=0.5 (see axis-48 walk-through) is exactly the kind of identity that, if it had an analogue between rank-kernel and concave-share-power axes, would collapse the partition. No such analogue exists in closed form — the rank-kernel functionals depend on the SORTED order of the sample, and the concave-share-power functionals depend on the SHARE values regardless of order. A distribution and any permutation of it give identical concave-share-power readings (they are permutation-invariant) but identical rank-kernel readings only because rank-kernel functionals re-sort first. So the two families are structurally distinct in the sense of operating on different invariants of the sample, and no closed-form bridge between them is possible without first going through the inverse-CDF, which is a non-algebraic operation. The collapse-B path is therefore closed.

So the four-class partition holds, the corpus witnesses it, and the closure of the rank-kernel family at v0.6.291 is genuine in the structural sense the inequality-stack non-redundancy invariant requires.

## 5. What axis-48 changes about the closure

Axis-48 (Chakravarty alpha=0.5, v0.6.292, sha 8d7b2a4) lands AFTER the rank-kernel family closes. It is in the concave-share-power family (alongside Atkinson axis-36), not the rank-kernel family. So axis-48 does not extend the rank-kernel taxonomy — it extends the concave-share-power taxonomy, and in particular it extends it with a NON-EDE-WRAPPED variant that the existing Atkinson axis does not provide. The full inequality stack as of v0.6.292 therefore carries TWO closed sub-families: the four-class rank-kernel family (axes 21, 43, 45, 47) and the two-axis concave-share-power family with parametric extensions (axes 36, 48). Plus the standalone axes that do not fit either family (Theil L/T/GE2 in the entropy family; Hoover, Pietra, Palma in the location-anchored family; Wolfson in the polarization family; Kolm-Pollak in the absolute-invariance family). The taxonomy as a whole is therefore a tree with one branching split at "rank-based vs share-based vs location-anchored vs polarization vs absolute-invariance" and second-level splits within each branch. Axis-48 is a leaf inside the share-based branch; the rank-based branch closed at v0.6.291.

## 6. Falsifiability and what it would take to break this read

The four-class partition is FALSIFIED if any of the following empirical claims is shown false:
- The S-Gini delta=2 cross-anchor identity to Gini holds at numerical-stability floor on every source (instrumented at sha 665e13f, currently green).
- The Bonferroni-Gini ratio is non-constant across the six corpus sources (live-smoke at v0.6.285 confirms a 2x range across sources).
- The Mehran-de-Vergottini difference is sign-symmetric only on symmetric distributions (axis-45 walk-through documents the asymmetric witness on the corpus).
- The S-Gini delta-monotonicity invariant fails on any source (instrumented at sha 665e13f, currently green).

If any of these turns red, the four-class partition is no longer empirically supported on this corpus and the rank-kernel family closure at v0.6.291 must be re-examined. The pre-merge gates on the inequality stack are designed to surface exactly these regressions, and the cross-anchor flags on each axis surface the bridge values directly to the user — no separate diagnostic axis is required.

The closure read is therefore: as of v0.6.291, the rank-kernel family on the pew-insights inequality stack carries four structurally distinct kernel functional forms (harmonic, linear, quadratic, polynomial parametric) at three landed scalar axes plus one parametric axis, with four cross-anchor identity bridges that pin the structural relationships between adjacent classes. The family is closed in the structural sense, and v0.6.292 (axis-48 Chakravarty) extends the inequality stack into a separately-closed concave-share-power family without disturbing the rank-kernel closure.
