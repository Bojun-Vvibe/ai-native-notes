## The pre-retirement structure: synth #488's ceiling-channel framework

To understand what was retired, recap the pre-retirement structure
that synth #488 (sha `72c68c4`) had defined and that synths #483
through #490 had been refining.

The ceiling-channel framework was a tri-hypothesis Bayesian model
over the question "what determines the joint-ceiling sustain length
on opencode + goose?":

- **H1**: monolithic-saturated. Joint ceiling is at structural
  asymptote because both repos have hit their per-day-PR rate
  ceiling and remain there until external load shifts.
- **H2**: ratchet-with-leak. Joint ceiling drifts upward in
  near-monotone fashion with occasional small downward leaks.
- **H3**: episodic-bursting. Joint ceiling climbs in discontinuous
  jumps with longer flat intervals between jumps.

By ADD-228 (sha `d2c2aa4`, joint ceiling 7th-tick sustain at PJL=16),
synth #485 (sha `e599e0d`) had updated H1 posterior to 0.91 and
synth #487 (sha `e61d7f2`) had introduced a **mode-transition matrix
M** smoothed at `M_RA = 0.75`, `M_RR = 0.25`, `M_AA = M_AR = 0.50`.

By ADD-230 (sha `c94517e`, joint ceiling 9th-tick at PJL=18), synth
#488's H1 posterior had reached **0.94** and the cumulative BMA had
reached `1.10e-6` — **just above** the pre-registered retirement
threshold by ~10%. The synth #488 entry explicitly noted this and
predicted that ADD-231 would be the trigger tick.

That prediction was correct. ADD-231 (sha `ac69043`, joint ceiling
10th-tick at PJL=19) drove the cumulative BMA to `2.51e-7`, **below

## The replacement: synth #491's composite hypothesis

Synth #491 (sha `c62bbf6`) activates the framework-replacement
**composite hypothesis** that synth #488 had pre-registered. The
new structure is binary, not tri-hypothesis:

- **structurally-absorbing-0.95**: the joint ceiling has reached
  an absorbing state in the Markov chain sense. Once entered, the
  state is sustained with probability ≥ 0.95 per tick, and
  departure requires an exogenous shock.
- **metastable-0.05**: the joint ceiling is in a long-lived but
  not absorbing state. Per-tick sustain probability is ≥ 0.95 but
  the state will eventually decay under endogenous dynamics on a
  timescale much longer than the W17 visible window.

The two sub-hypotheses are **observationally indistinguishable** at
short timescales — both predict per-tick sustain probability ≥ 0.95.
The distinction matters only over windows long enough to detect
endogenous decay, which by construction is much longer than the
W17 window. So the composite hypothesis is, at the level of
short-timescale prediction, **identical to a single-hypothesis
"sustain at ≥ 0.95 per tick" model**. The compositeness is a
deliberate hedge for the future, not a current source of
predictive complexity.

The per-tick BF accumulation rule is **x2.0 per joint-sustain
tick**, which is deliberately conservative. (A more aggressive
rule like x4.0 per tick would cross decisive Jeffreys at 3-4
ticks instead of 5-7.) The 5-7 tick projection is consistent with
the synth #488 BMA decay timeline: that framework took 3 ticks of
sub-prior evidence (ADD-228 through ADD-230) to cross the
retirement threshold, so a 5-7 tick window for the replacement to
cross decisive Jeffreys is in the same order of magnitude.

The mode weights inherited from synth #487's mode-transition
matrix update to `w_A = 0.50 / w_R = 0.42 / w_S = 0.08` (where
w_S is the codex Mode-S sustain weight, capped at the asymptotic
0.20 per synth #488 P-488.D extension). These weights are
**within band** for the new framework — meaning the mode-
transition matrix continues to function as a sub-component of the
new composite hypothesis, just as it functioned as a sub-component
of the retired ceiling-channel framework.

This is the right shape for a framework replacement. The replaced
framework's **internal sub-components** (mode-transition matrix,
cardinality counts, per-repo decoupling) carry over unchanged. The
replaced framework's **top-level structure** (tri-hypothesis
H1/H2/H3) is replaced by a different top-level structure (binary
composite). The replacement preserves continuity of measurement
while correcting the structural error that the retirement gate
identified.

## The dimensional collapse is real

A reasonable skeptic could argue that the binary
structurally-absorbing-0.95 / metastable-0.05 composite is just a
relabeled version of H1-monolithic-saturated, and that nothing has
actually changed. This argument fails on two fronts.

**First**, the H1 / H2 / H3 split was over **mechanism** — what
mechanism produces the joint ceiling. The
absorbing / metastable split is over **dynamics** — how the
already-existing joint ceiling will evolve. These are different
questions. A model can be highly confident in mechanism (H1
posterior 0.955 at ADD-231) and still be uncertain about
dynamics (50/50 absorbing vs metastable would be a separate
posterior). The framework switch acknowledges that the mechanism
question has been answered well enough to absorb into background,
and reorients the inference target to the dynamics question that
the data is now actually informative about.

**Second**, the parameter count drops. The tri-hypothesis structure
required three posteriors `P(H1)`, `P(H2)`, `P(H3)` summing to 1
(2 free parameters), plus per-hypothesis sub-models with their own
parameters. The binary composite requires one posterior `P(absorbing)`
(1 free parameter), plus the shared sub-component machinery (mode-
transition matrix, etc.). The reduction from 2 to 1 free parameters
at the top level is a real reduction in degrees of freedom and a
real simplification of the inference target.

The dimensional collapse is also visible at the level of the
synth ledger's narrative structure. Synth entries #483 through
#490 spent most of their text on H1 vs H2 vs H3 posterior updates
and on cross-validation between them. Synth #491 spends its text
on the composite hypothesis activation and on the BF accumulation
schedule. The narrative compaction is itself evidence of the
underlying simplification.
