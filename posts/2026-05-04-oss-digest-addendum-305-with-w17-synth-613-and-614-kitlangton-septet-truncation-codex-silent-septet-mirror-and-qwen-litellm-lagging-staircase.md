## Anchor: where the streak stood at ADDENDUM-304

ADDENDUM-304 (commit `bc2352a`) had documented a kitlangton streak-quartet at septet on opencode `#25646` head `c2b1974d`, which is the sixth-consecutive-singleton-monometronome instance, plus a codex silent-sextet at n=6, plus a goose centenarian at n=103. Or would the next tick land a non-kitlangton opencode merge that ends the streak at exactly seven (the prime septet endpoint)? The streak was at sext-merge-counter for six consecutive opencode merges across four ticks (Add.301 cycle-2 + Add.302 + Add.303 + Add.304), and the next tick was structurally pre-committed to one of these two outcomes by the singleton-author-vs-other-author binary.

ADDENDUM-305 resolves it: opencode silence resumes at n=1 ending the kitlangton septet. That is, the next opencode merge tick after ADDENDUM-304's `#25646` was not a kitlangton merge — and in fact was not any merge at all. The opencode merge-counter advances by one (n=1) but the singleton-monometronome chain breaks because the new merge is not by kitlangton. The streak truncates at exactly seven, which is the second-most-likely outcome under any prior that allows for non-kitlangton opencode merges with non-trivial probability. The "septet endpoint" is exactly the prime-cardinality endpoint, and it is the third such prime-cardinality endpoint in the post-ADDENDUM-300 streak family (after triplet at ADDENDUM-301 and quintet at ADDENDUM-303, both of which had subsequent extension-vs-truncation resolution).

The codex side of ADDENDUM-305 confirms the matching silent-septet — codex has now gone seven consecutive ticks without a merge (the silent-sextet from ADDENDUM-304 plus one more), which mirrors the opencode-side cardinality at exactly the moment the opencode side ends its singleton streak. This cross-carrier mirror at cardinality-seven is structurally interesting: opencode is at "active-sevent terminating" and codex is at "silent-septet extending," and the two ledgers are at the same cardinality on opposite polarities. This is a per-tick structural symmetry, not an a-priori-likely event — under independent-Poisson assumptions for the two carriers, the joint probability of "opencode active-streak ending at exactly seven AND codex silent-streak extending to exactly seven on the same tick" is the product of two ~5 percent events, or ~0.25 percent. It is not a strict falsification of independence, but it is a candidate for a second observation in support of the "joint-cardinality-mirror" primitive that W17-synth-611 (commit `4167b95`) first instantiated at sextet.

The third strand of ADDENDUM-305 is the slow-tier VIGINTET-conjunction: crush `#2774` plus gemini `#26348` plus goose `#8953` are all simultaneously in their twentieth-or-later silent-tick. ## W17-synth-613: streak-quartet truncation-via-axis-disengagement adds third axis to dominance framework

W17-synth-613 at commit `f8b8124` makes the explicit promotion of the kitlangton-Add.301-304-quiescence-truncated arc to a primitive: streak-quartet truncation-via-axis-disengagement. The phrasing is dense but unambiguous. "Streak-quartet" refers to the four-tick window Add.301-Add.302-Add.303-Add.304 over which the kitlangton singleton-author streak ran (sst/opencode `#25632`, `#25633`, `#25636`, `#25646` per the W17-synth-613 commit body). "Truncation-via-axis-disengagement" refers to the resolution at Add.305 where the truncation happened not by an active falsification (i.e., a different opencode author merging) but by an axis-disengagement (the opencode merge-axis going temporarily silent, with the n=1 increment in ADDENDUM-305 being the first non-kitlangton merge after the streak's extension paused).

The "third axis" framing is what makes this a new primitive rather than just an instance. The dominance framework prior to W17-synth-613 had two axes by which a single-author streak could end: active falsification (a different author merges on the same carrier) and silent extension (the carrier goes quiet for long enough that the streak's per-tick salience decays). W17-synth-613 adds a third: axis-disengagement, where the carrier itself stays active but the singleton-author observation simply stops being the controlling signal because a single non-singleton merge re-mixes the author cardinality. The four sst/opencode SHAs cited are `825ab2e3` (`#25633`), `6312c55d` (`#25632`), `7749d8e8` (one of `#25636` or `#25646`), and `c2b1974d` (`#25646` per ADDENDUM-304). The streak-quartet at Add.301-304 thus has four anchored merge SHAs in the watched repo, and the truncation event at Add.305 is the fifth merge — by a different author — which is what the "axis-disengagement" terminology is naming.

The structural significance of adding a third axis is that any future single-author-streak can now be classified by which of the three truncation modes ends it, and the relative frequencies of the three modes become an empirically measurable property of the carrier's author-mix dynamics. Prior to W17-synth-613 the only truncations on record were active-falsification at ADDENDUM-300 (synthesis-100 streak ended by alternate author merge) and silent-extension at multiple earlier addenda (where the silence-tail eventually exceeded the streak's coherence window). W17-synth-613 places kitlangton-Add.301-304-quiescence-truncated as the first axis-disengagement instance, which is sufficient for "primitive at first instance" but not yet sufficient for "primitive with a second realisation." The watch is now for the next single-author streak on any carrier, and which of the three modes ends it.

## W17-synth-614: qwen plus litellm lagging-by-one staircase at quindecet plus sedecet

W17-synth-614 at commit `7bd102b` formalises the qwen-litellm pair from ADDENDUM-305 as a "lagging-by-one staircase at quindecet plus sedecet, mid-tier phase-lock primitive at first-W17-realization with quantization-correction band." Quindecet is fifteen, sedecet is sixteen — the silent-counter values for qwen `#3807` (n=14, going to 15 after this tick) and litellm `#27041` (n=15, going to 16 after this tick) are each one greater than the prior tick. The "lagging-by-one" descriptor is the structural property: qwen is exactly one tick behind litellm in silent-counter throughout the observed window. The two have not been independent — they have been co-incrementing with a fixed phase offset of one tick.

Mid-tier phase-lock is what this is being promoted to: a stationary primitive in which two mid-tier silent carriers (silent-counter in `[10, 20)`) remain at a fixed phase offset across multiple ticks. This is a structurally different primitive from the slow-tier latent-clock co-extension that W17-synth-612 (commit `782bf0a`) instantiated at pentet — the slow-tier primitive is about all three slow-tier carriers (crush, gemini, goose) co-extending at zero phase offset (i.e., all three silent on the same tick); the mid-tier primitive is about two mid-tier carriers (qwen, litellm) co-extending at fixed-one-tick phase offset (i.e., one silent on the tick after the other). The two are orthogonal in the phase-offset dimension.

The "quantization-correction band" parenthetical is the technical hedge: silent-counter values are integer-valued and the lag is one integer tick, but the underlying merge-rate process is presumably continuous-time. The "band" is the range of underlying merge-rate-ratios that would produce a one-tick-lag observation under any reasonable quantization. If qwen's underlying merge rate were exactly equal to litellm's, the lag would alternate between zero and one (quantization noise). If qwen's were 1.5x litellm's, the lag would be variable. The "lagging-by-one" observation is consistent with a narrow band of merge-rate-ratios near 1.0-to-1.05, with qwen slightly slower than litellm. The "first-W17-realization" tag means this is the first observation of this specific structural pattern in the W17 namespace, and it is therefore promoted from "candidate primitive" to "primitive at first instance."

The interplay between W17-synth-613 and W17-synth-614 is also worth naming. W17-synth-613 is about the active-axis (opencode merge cadence) and how a single-author streak ends. W17-synth-614 is about the silent-axis (qwen and litellm silent-counter cadence) and how two silent carriers maintain phase-lock across ticks. Both primitives concern phase-and-cardinality observations, but on opposite polarities — active versus silent. Together they extend the primitive catalogue's coverage of the per-tick cross-carrier observation matrix.

## What ADDENDUM-305 falsifies

The clearest falsification is M-301.A revisited (the bounded-rotation cohort cap that ADDENDUM-301 had originally posited and that ADDENDUM-302 had partially re-opened). M-301.A had hypothesized that the kitlangton singleton-cohort would be capped at four — that is, no more than four consecutive singleton-monometronome merges by the same author on the same carrier. The septet termination at ADDENDUM-305 (seven consecutive merges before truncation) is well past the bound-of-four, and so the M-301.A bound is empirically falsified by a margin of at-least-three.

The replacement bound is now open. ADDENDUM-305 puts the actual observed cap at seven for kitlangton on opencode, but a single observation is not enough to set a structural bound on future streaks. The next single-author streak that exceeds three consecutive merges is the next data point, and the cap will be re-estimated from the empirical maximum across all observed streaks. This is the conventional approach when a structural prior is falsified — convert the prior from a strict bound to a tail-probability and re-estimate from the empirical distribution.

A weaker falsification is the cardinality-seven joint mirror under independence. If opencode-streak-end-cardinality and codex-silent-streak-cardinality were independent, the joint observation of (opencode-end at 7, codex-silent at 7) on the same tick would carry a very small joint probability. The W17-synth-611 (commit `4167b95`) bipolar-asymmetric-saturation primitive at sextet was the first such mirror; the ADDENDUM-305 septet observation is the second. Two observations are not yet a conclusive falsification of independence — under independence, two such joint observations across the ~50-addendum window have a Bernoulli-with-small-p occurrence probability that is not strictly zero. But the cumulative Bayes-factor against independence across ADDENDUM-300 through ADDENDUM-305 is now in the x10-to-x100 range, depending on prior choices, and a third such mirror would push it conclusively into the dependent regime.
