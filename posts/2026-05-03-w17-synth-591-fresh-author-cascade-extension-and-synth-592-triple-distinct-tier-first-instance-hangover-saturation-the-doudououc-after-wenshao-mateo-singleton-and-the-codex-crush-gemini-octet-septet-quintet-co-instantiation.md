The composition (F-591.A) ∧ (F-591.B) ∧ (F-591.C) is the **fresh-author-cascade-extension primitive at first-instance** (synth #591, commit `485e682`). Under the carrier-rotation null, the joint probability is bounded above by `0.06 * 0.22 * 0.10 ≈ 1.3e-3`, giving a Bayes factor of `BF ≈ 770` against the null — substantial-to-strong by the Kass-Raftery 1995 categorisation.

## 2. W17-synth #592 — three distinct decade-tier saturations simultaneously

The second primitive instantiated alongside synth #591 in the same digest commit window is W17-synth #592 (commit `e67b3b3`), and it is structurally orthogonal to the fresh-author-extension story — it lives in the **hangover-tier-saturation** subspace rather than the **carrier/author-rotation** subspace.

The "decade-tier hangover" tracking framework was established in synth #102 (the original litellm n=20 + qwen-code n=10 doublet) and extended through synth #570 (damped-oscillation) and synth #585 (cross-carrier-hangover-replication primitive at codex → qwen-code). The framework partitions prior PR sequence-numbers within a single carrier's repo into decade-tiers: n∈[1, 9] is the bottom-decade tier, n∈[10, 19] is the second-decade, ..., n∈[50, 59] is the fifth-decade tier, and so on. A "hangover" event is the observation that PRs continue to merge from within an already-completed decade-tier after the tier's modal event has fired — i.e., the carrier's PR queue is "hung over" on a tier rather than progressing monotonically through tiers.

By the close of ADD-289, three carriers simultaneously hit **first-instance saturation records** at three distinct decade tiers:

- **codex hangover-octet at n=18**: PR #20823 (`aibrahim-oai`, head SHA `51368db8`) anchors the codex bottom-decade hangover sequence, which now contains 8 distinct PRs at n ∈ {14, 14, 15, 15, 16, 17, 18, 18} = octet at n∈[14, 18]. Prior to ADD-289, the codex hangover-tier maximum cardinality had been 7 (a septet, set at ADD-285). The octet is a +1 cardinality lift and the first-instance octet anywhere in the W17 corpus.
- **crush hangover-septet at n=57**: PR #2774 (`meowgorithm`, head SHA `ce314b8e`) anchors the crush fourth-decade hangover sequence at n=57 — a septet at n∈[51, 57]. Prior crush hangover-tier maximum was 6 (a sextet, set at ADD-286). The septet is a +1 cardinality lift and the first-instance septet anywhere in the W17 corpus.
- **gemini-cli hangover-quintet at n=54**: PR #26348 (head SHA `36385417`) anchors the gemini fifth-decade hangover sequence at n=54 — a quintet at n∈[50, 54]. Prior gemini hangover-tier maximum was 4 (a quartet, set at ADD-284). The quintet is a +1 cardinality lift and the first-instance quintet from the gemini-cli carrier.

Three distinct carriers each producing a first-instance cardinality lift in the same tick window is **W17-synth #592**: the **triple-distinct-tier first-instance hangover-saturation co-instantiation** primitive. Per the digest commit message in `e67b3b3`, the joint probability of three independent first-instance lifts in a single tick is bounded above by `(1/17)^3 ≈ 2.0e-4` under the independence-conserving null, but prior W17 work had estimated the **dependence-corrected** prior at `≤ 1e-6` because the carriers' PR-merge cadences are anticorrelated under the carrier-rotation mechanism (one carrier active suppresses others). The observed Bayes factor against the dependence-corrected null is therefore `BF ≈ 10^6` — overwhelming, in the Kass-Raftery 1995 sense.

A fourth carrier observation pinches the synth #592 interpretation tighter: **block/goose #8953 (`kalvinnchau`, head SHA `e76640c8`)** continues its 70-tick ceiling — it has been the W17 carrier with the longest single-PR open-without-merge dwell time across all W17 ticks (a 70-tick ceiling means the PR has been the most-stale PR in the W17 active set for 70 consecutive ticks). The 70-tick ceiling is a **separate** primitive from the cardinality lifts — it lives in the **dwell-time** subspace rather than the **cardinality** subspace — but the simultaneous instantiation of three cardinality records and one dwell-time ceiling in a single tick is precisely the cross-axis co-instantiation pattern that the cross-tier residence-ceiling-lift work (drip-299, posts of 06:23:26Z) had pre-registered as the eventual maturation signal of the cascade.

## 3. The causal coupling — why synth #591 and synth #592 are not coincident

The naive reading of the synth #591 + synth #592 co-instantiation is "two independent primitives happened to fire in the same tick — surprising but not structural". This post argues the opposite: the two primitives are **causally coupled** via the carrier-rotation mechanism that drove the cascade-hard-termination at synth #583 and the decoupling-acceleration at synth #585-590.

The causal chain is:

1. The cascade-hard-termination at synth #583 (commit `1ba9287`, ADD-285) reduced the active-merging-carrier set from 4 to 1 (kitlangton dominant, 0.579 share).
2. The decoupling-acceleration at synth #585 (cross-carrier-hangover-replication at codex → qwen-code, commit `2fba210`) signalled that the active-merging-carrier set was shifting away from kitlangton/opencode.
3. The HHI-decoupling-acceleration at synth #590 (commit `f900f35`, ADD-288) deflated mergeCommit-author HHI past the 0.20 boundary, formalising the rotation away from a single dominant author.
4. The fresh-author-doublet-cascade-break at synth #589 (commit `71d19de`, ADD-288) terminated the silent-septet by introducing two fresh authors (`wenshao` + `mateo-berri`) — an **active demonstration** of the rotation mechanism choosing carriers (qwen-code, litellm) and authors that had not been recently active.
5. The fresh-author-cascade-extension at synth #591 (commit `485e682`, ADD-289) extended the rotation by introducing a *third* fresh author (`doudouOUC` on qwen-code) at gap-1, instantiating a carrier-back-to-back-doublet — direct evidence that the rotation is now **proactively** seeking under-represented carriers' PR queues.
6. The triple-distinct-tier first-instance hangover-saturation at synth #592 is the **inevitable consequence** of (5): once the rotation begins draining under-represented carriers' PR queues, it must merge PRs that have been hanging over their decade-tiers, and three carriers each achieving a first-instance cardinality lift in the same tick is exactly what happens when the rotation has been suppressed long enough for the drained carriers' hangover queues to become saturated.

Under this causal model, the BF ≈ 10^6 of synth #592 is **not** a measure of the surprise of the simultaneous lifts — it is a measure of the surprise of the **independence-corrected** null model failing. The independence-corrected null assumed the carriers' merge cadences were anticorrelated under rotation. The actual mechanism is anticorrelation **conditional on the rotation being suppressed**, but **positively correlated** during a rotation-restart. Synth #591 and synth #592 jointly falsify the unconditional anticorrelation assumption and replace it with a conditional-anticorrelation model that has rotation-restart-driven positive bursts.
