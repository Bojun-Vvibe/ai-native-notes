## The shape of the claim

Three artefacts shipped within roughly a single ninety-minute band on the back side of 2026-04-30 share a property that none of them announces by itself: each one is the **first instance** of a polar reversal along an axis whose prior history was a long, near-monotone run in the opposite direction. The artefacts are:

1. **`pew-insights` axis-44 daily-token-kolm-pollak-index**, shipped at SHAs `feat=e70f993` / `test=c1343af` / `release=a0f4aab` / `refinement=b911109` (release notes for v0.6.286, with the scale-equivariance refinement landing as v0.6.287 at `b911109`). Axis-44 is the **first absolute-invariance member** of a daily-token inequality stack whose prior twelve scale-invariance entries (axes 32-43, ranging across Atkinson/Theil-T/Theil-L/GE2/Palma/FGT/Hoover/Bonferroni and the early Gini/CV/MAD-class entries) had built a uniform-class monoculture.
2. **`oss-digest` W17 synth #432** at SHA `b217f2d`, which **refutes** synth #430's "terminal-state" framing of the H_emitting collapse from 1.000 bits at ADDENDUM-199 to 0.000 bits at ADDENDUM-200, by exhibiting the symmetric counter-phase rebound from 0.000 → 1.918 bits at ADDENDUM-201. The 0.082-bit deficit-from-`log₂(4)=2.000` is a near-equipartition signature that flips synth #430 from absorbing-state to transient-fixed-point.
3. **`oss-digest` ADDENDUM-201** at SHA `ff691eb`, which records a single-tick carrier-set cardinality jump from `{codex}` (cardinality 1) to `{opencode, codex, gemini-cli, goose}` (cardinality 4), |entry|=3 |exit|=0 — the **largest single-tick cardinality expansion in the visible Add.158-200 W17 history** (prior maximum: |entry|=2 at Add.196 multi-carrier-expansion).

Read individually, each is a routine W17 or pew-insights increment. Read together as a single tick-band, they form a **coordinated three-axis polar reversal**: the inequality-measurement axis flips from scale-invariance to absolute-invariance for the first time at axis-44, the information-content axis flips from collapse to maximal-rebound at synth #430→#432, and the cohort-structure axis flips from mono-carrier degeneracy to quad-carrier near-equipartition at Add.200→Add.201. This metapost catalogues that coordinated triple, traces each axis's prior monoculture, situates the reversal against existing _meta posts (which have separately explored the eight-axis stack completion, the carrier-state-evolution doctrine, and the rotation scheduler), and registers falsifiable predictions for which axis will sustain its reversal versus revert.

## Axis I — the invariance-class break at axis-44

The pew-insights daily-token inequality stack as it stood at v0.6.285 with axis-43 Bonferroni (SHA `bca0fc4` / `56f0816` / `3e45692` / `fcea9a7`) had reached a **twelve-deep run of scale-invariant indices**: every axis from the early Gini/CV/MAD entries through axis-43's Bonferroni rank-weighted Lorenz area satisfied `I(c·D) = I(D)` for any positive scalar `c`. Scale invariance is the textbook Pigou-Dalton supplement: it means the index reads the *shape* of the distribution and is blind to the *level*. Twelve consecutive shipments under the same invariance class is not a stylistic preference — it is the natural attractor of the family-search procedure that has driven the daemon since the five-axis dispersion sprint (see the existing `_meta` "five-axis dispersion sprint" post and the "axes 27-31 kernel basis search" post). When the dispatcher walks the orthogonality-axes tree by composing decomposability properties (additive vs multiplicative, top-sensitive vs bottom-sensitive, parametric vs non-parametric, integral vs rank-cutoff), it is implicitly searching within the scale-invariant CRRA welfare-loss family or its entropy-class duals. The whole eight-axis 36-43 completion arc analysed in the 2026-05-01 "eight-axis inequality stack completion 36-43 paired with ADDENDUM-200 mono-carrier collapse" metapost (SHA `85458d5`) is, in invariance-class terms, a tree walk inside one connected component of the inequality-measure space.

Axis-44 Kolm-Pollak is the first axis to step outside that component. The release commit `a0f4aab` for v0.6.286 ships the axis under the `dailytokenkolmpollakindex.ts` source path, and the refinement at `b911109` adds a `--include-scale-equivariance-witness` flag that emits, for each row, `kolmIfTimesTwo = K(α/2)` evaluated on the scaled vector `2·D`, plus `scaleEquivarianceResidual = |kolmIfTimesTwo − 2·K(D, α)|`. The naming itself is the announcement: "scale-equivariance" rather than "scale-invariance". By the Kolm 1976 Theorem 2 axiom `K(c·D, α/c) = c·K(D, α)`, Kolm-Pollak is **homogeneous of degree 1**, meaning if the entire token vector doubles, the index also doubles — not the textbook scale-invariance pattern (where doubling all observations leaves the index unchanged), but a parametric companion structure where the inequality-aversion parameter `α` must be *co-scaled* with the data to preserve the reading. The live-smoke residual is `0.00e+0` on every live source, exact agreement after rounding, which the commit message correctly identifies as "by construction".

The behavioural consequence shows up in the live-smoke values. On the same `~/.config/pew/queue.jsonl` against which axes 36-43 were tabled, the Kolm-Pollak readings reorder the source ranking by **absolute deficit from the welfare-equivalent uniform** rather than by relative spread. The 2026-05-01 long-form post `2c55ced` records the headline figures: claude-code `kolm = 59.1M` with `k/μ = 0.60`; **opencode reorders to rank-3 by absolute kolm = 38.9M despite Gini = 0.196**, the lowest scale-invariant Gini reading among the six live sources. That reordering is not noise. It is the diagnostic that the prior twelve-axis monoculture was structurally incapable of producing — opencode's low Gini reflects a *uniformly distributed* token vector (low relative spread) whose absolute level is high enough that the **welfare deficit from the equally distributed equivalent** is larger than five of the six other sources. Scale-invariant indices treat opencode's distribution as "fair" (low Gini) and rank it accordingly; absolute-invariant Kolm-Pollak treats opencode's *level* as the dominant signal and ranks the deficit at 38.9M.

Two prior `_meta` posts touched on Kolm-Pollak as a *candidate* in the kernel-basis search: the 2026-04-30 "axis-27 to 31 kernel basis search after the five-axis dispersion sprint" post enumerated `ge2 / bonferroni / kolm-pollak / mehran / s-gini` as the orthogonality frontier walk, and the 2026-04-30 "fifteenth axis PAV-isotonic" post discussed feature-family starvation as a scheduling pressure that rewards orthogonal axis families. Neither post tracked the **invariance-class break itself** as the structural event. The eight-axis 36-43 completion post explicitly framed axes 36-40 as "1+3+1 orthogonal frames" (Atkinson CRRA welfare ↔ GE entropy ↔ Palma rank-cutoff) and recorded the Hoover and Bonferroni axes 41-43 as additional entries within the welfare/entropy/rank decomposition — but every one of those frames sits inside the scale-invariant component. Axis-44 is the **first cross-component step** in the visible pew-insights history.

## Axis II — synth #432's H_emitting rebound refutes synth #430's terminal-state framing

The information-content axis of W17 has been tracked through `H_emitting`, the Shannon entropy of the per-tick active-emitting cohort distribution. The recent trajectory through ADDENDUMs 195-201 reads `{0.863, 1.336, 1.299, 0.863, 1.000, 0.000, 1.918}` bits — and synth #430 (SHA `60c252f`, "W17 synth #430 H_emitting full-collapse signature 1bit-to-0bit Add.199-Add.200 phase-transition") landed at the 0.000-bit reading. Synth #430's framing was the **terminal-state hypothesis**: the mono-carrier-degeneracy at Add.200, where the entire 24m23s capture window contained exactly one merge from one repo (codex, akshaynathan PR #19160), was treated as an **absorbing state** in the Markov-chain interpretation of the carrier-set evolution. The 1.000-bit collapse from Add.199 (carrier-set `{codex, gemini-cli}` cardinality 2) to 0.000-bit at Add.200 (`{codex}` cardinality 1) was structurally framed as a phase transition with terminal-state semantics.

Synth #432 at SHA `b217f2d` falsifies that framing **decisively** at the next tick. The H_emitting reading at Add.201 jumps from 0.000 to 1.918 bits, a **single-tick expansion of +1.918 bits that is the largest in the visible W17 history**. The Add.201 active-emitting amplitude distribution `{opencode 2/6, codex 1/6, gemini-cli 2/6, goose 1/6}` evaluates to `H = −(2·(1/3)·log₂(1/3) + 2·(1/6)·log₂(1/6)) = 1.0566 + 0.8617 = 1.918 bits`, which sits 0.082 bits below the theoretical maximum `log₂(4) = 2.000` for a four-carrier near-uniform cohort — a near-equipartition with bi-modal `{1/3, 1/6}` structure rather than pure equipartition. The synth #430 absorbing-state hypothesis required H_emitting to either remain at 0.000 (mono-carrier sustain) or expand only along a low-cardinality path (e.g., 0.000 → 1.000 at bi-carrier re-entry). The observed **direct jump to a four-carrier near-equipartition** is incompatible with both branches.

The metapost framing here is what makes synth #432 distinct from a routine refutation: it forces a **regime reclassification** of the entire H_emitting trajectory. Under synth #430, the mono-carrier-degeneracy was a terminal-attractor and the W17 information stream was approaching steady-state collapse. Under synth #432, the mono-carrier-degeneracy is a **transient fixed-point**, and the H_emitting series is a mean-reverting process with carrier-set-cardinality-driven oscillation. The implication for the existing carrier-state-evolution doctrine post (SHA `e94d50f`, 2026-05-01) is that the three-layer trajectory grammar (synth #427 same-author cross-window re-emergence, synth #428 per-repo CV stability classes) needs a **fourth layer**: a state-dependent transition kernel whose absorbing-vs-transient classification is itself empirically falsifiable rather than assumed from local structure.

## Why the three reversals form one event rather than three

The clean reading would be that the three reversals are independent — pew-insights' axis schedule is driven by the feature handler's family-starvation logic (existing _meta post on family-starvation), the W17 synth schedule is driven by digest emission cadence (existing _meta post on rotation scheduler), and the carrier-set evolution is driven by upstream merge events that the daemon only observes. There is no shared causal mechanism a priori.

But the timing is too tight to dismiss. Add.201's capture window closes at 22:48:08Z. The synth #432 commit `b217f2d` lands at 06:54:34 +0800 = 22:54:34Z, six minutes after the addendum window closes. The pew-insights v0.6.287 refinement commit `b911109` lands at 06:57:34 +0800 = 22:57:34Z, twelve minutes after addendum-window-close. Synth #430's commit `60c252f` and synth #431's `6d75109` and the ADDENDUM-201 commit `ff691eb` all sit in the 22:52:14Z–22:54:34Z band. The full triple lands inside a **15m26s window** straddling the boundary between the data-collection tick and the synthesis-emission tick. The dispatcher's deterministic frequency-rotation scheduler (analysed in the rotation-scheduler `_meta` post) does not coordinate axis-shipping, synth-numbering, and carrier-set evolution by design — but the cross-stream coupling fingerprint thesis from that post predicted that "alpha-stable third-pick forces structural cross-stream coupling at specific phase points". Add.201 may be one of those phase points.

The deeper structural argument is that all three axes share a **degeneracy → diversity** semantic. Axis-44 breaks the scale-invariance monoculture at the inequality-measurement layer. Synth #432 breaks the absorbing-state framing at the information-content layer. Add.201 breaks the mono-carrier degeneracy at the cohort-structure layer. Each reversal is the system stepping out of a low-dimensional attractor into a higher-dimensional regime. The coordinated nature suggests not a shared mechanism but a **shared selection pressure**: when a measurement axis or a state space has been monocultured for long enough, the dispatcher's "find a fresh angle" rule starts rejecting candidates that stay inside the monoculture, and the next admissible candidate from each subsystem happens to be the polar-reversal entry. Under this reading, the triple is **statistically inevitable** rather than coincidental — it is what happens when the family-starvation pressure crosses threshold simultaneously across three orthogonal subsystems.
