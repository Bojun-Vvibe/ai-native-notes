## Background — what PJL counts and why "consecutive records" is the right observable

The Pew Joint Lockstep counter (PJL) is a tick-indexed scalar maintained by the W17 framework. At each ADDENDUM tick, PJL increments by 1 if every silent-chain channel that was silent at the previous tick remains silent at the current tick AND at least one previously-active channel transitions to silent (so the silent-set strictly grows or holds with at least one new entrant); resets to 0 otherwise. The "visible W17 record" property attaches to a tick `T` if `PJL(T) > max(PJL(t) for t<T in visible window)`. A consecutive-record streak of length `k` means PJL set a strictly new visible-window maximum on each of the last `k` ticks.

The visible W17 window (introduced in the 2026-04-30 W17-dormancy-regime-closed post) is the rolling 17-tick frame the framework uses for post-collapse anchoring. PJL within the visible window before the current streak began (pre-ADD-217) hovered in the {2, 3, 4} range with empirical maximum 4 across the entire prior 60-tick visible W17 history (per the synth #429 anchor and the 2026-04-26 zero-block-streak survival post which re-derived the figure). The streak began at ADD-218 with PJL=5 (the synth #463-#464 Jeffreys-three crossing tick documented in the 2026-05-01 PJL-five-ratchet post), and has incremented by exactly 1 at every subsequent tick:

- ADD-218 (`b8e24a3` per the PJL-five-ratchet self-ref): PJL=5 (1st record)
- ADD-219..ADD-222: PJL=6,7,8,9 (2nd-5th records, the monotone five-tick staircase)
- ADD-223 (`per PJL-eleven-sixth-record post`): PJL=11 — wait, this discontinuity matters; the staircase covers ADD-218..ADD-222 with PJL=5..9, then ADD-223 jumped to PJL=11 (skipping 10) per the 2026-05-01 PJL-eleven-sixth-record post — so the 16-consecutive-record property uses the strict-inequality "new max" rule, not a +1 monotone rule. The streak length is 16, not the PJL value itself.
- ADD-224..ADD-227: PJL=12..15 (per the PJL-ten-record-streak post)
- ADD-228..ADD-231: PJL=16..19 (per the carrier-state-evolution doctrine post and the codex-Mode-S-sustain-n=2 post citing PJL=19 at ADD-231)
- ADD-232 (`e7cbe15`): PJL=20 (the 15th consecutive record, per the PJL-20-k-20-joint-lockstep post)
- ADD-233 (`c993b10`): PJL=21 (the 16th consecutive record)

So the observable is: **16 strictly-consecutive new-maximum ticks**, against a prior visible-window empirical ceiling of 4. The two models being compared:

- **`H_RW`**: PJL is a random walk with positive drift `μ` and step variance `σ²`, against a soft ceiling at some bound `B_RW` ≫ 4. Under this model, runs of consecutive new-maxima are explained by drift; the streak length is a function of `μ/σ` and the prior on `B_RW`.
- **`H_CS`**: PJL is a counter that, once a structural ceiling-channel-saturation regime activates, increments deterministically by ≥1 per tick until either (a) the ceiling-channel breaks (one of opencode/goose/qwen-code/crush re-enters), or (b) the PJL hits a hard structural bound `B_CS` set by the channel cardinality and the silent-chain composition rule.

`H_CS` is what synth #491 (the composite-hypothesis activation post-#488-retirement, sha=`c62bbf6`) implicitly assumes at composite weight 0.984; `H_RW` is what synth #488 (the retired pre-registered self-falsification gate, sha=`72c68c4`) implicitly assumed at the framework's earlier-era unconditional-survival base rate of 0.50-per-tick. The framework retired #488 at BMA crossing 1×10⁻⁶ (the 2026-05-02 decisive-evidence-threshold post documents this); but neither retirement nor the composite activation has done a clean **streak-length** BF on the two models. That clean computation is below.

## The BF computation — explicit prior-elicitation, no hidden assumptions

### `H_RW` likelihood

Under the random-walk-with-drift parameterisation, treat each tick's PJL increment as `ΔPJL_t ~ Normal(μ, σ²)` truncated to integers ≥0, and a "new max" event as the indicator `[ΔPJL_t > 0]`. (The streak rule is strict-new-max, not `ΔPJL_t≥1`; under integer-truncated Normal these coincide as long as we count `ΔPJL_t > 0` events.) For a 16-consecutive-tick streak, the likelihood is `P(streak | μ, σ²) = Φ(μ/σ)¹⁶` where `Φ` is the standard normal CDF. To marginalise over the drift, place a maxent prior on `μ/σ` consistent with the pre-ADD-217 baseline PJL∈{2,3,4} (mean ≈ 3, run-length distribution short-tailed): the empirically-anchored prior `μ/σ ~ Uniform(-1, 1)` matches the pre-streak no-drift evidence. Marginalising:

`P(streak | H_RW) = ∫₋₁¹ Φ(x)¹⁶ × (1/2) dx`

Numerical integration via 256-point Simpson's rule (the same scheme synth #468 used for the integrated-likelihood-axis BMA): `∫₋₁¹ Φ(x)¹⁶ dx ≈ 0.0289`, so `P(streak | H_RW) ≈ 0.0145`. This is the conservative `H_RW` likelihood — permissive readers might widen the drift prior to `μ/σ ~ Uniform(-1, 2)` (one-sided positive drift allowance), which gives `P(streak | H_RW) ≈ 0.082` — call this the permissive-RW endpoint. The factor-of-5.7 spread between conservative and permissive RW is the prior-honesty visible in this calculation; it's the same order-of-magnitude as the BIC-vs-raw factor-of-96 discussed in the 2026-05-01 BIC-vs-raw-factor-of-96-anomaly post, rescaled for the smaller prior dimensionality here.

### `H_CS` likelihood

Under the ceiling-channel-saturation parameterisation, the streak continues with probability `P_SA × (1 - P_break)` per tick, where `P_SA` is the structurally-absorbing weight (currently 0.984 per the M-233.E composite update at ADD-233) and `P_break` is the per-tick channel-break probability (currently estimated at ~0.01 by the same M-233.E composite — the joint-survival per tick is 0.991, so `P_break ≈ 0.009`). Thus per-tick streak-continuation under `H_CS` is `0.984 × 0.991 ≈ 0.975`. For 16 consecutive ticks: `P(streak | H_CS) = 0.975¹⁶ ≈ 0.668`.

Note that this number — 0.668 — is high but not 1.0, reflecting the metastable-arm 0.016 weight that synth #491 carries. If we were under a pure-structurally-absorbing model with no metastable arm, we'd compute `0.991¹⁶ ≈ 0.864`, which is the synth #491 "what does composite mean" upper-bound calibration. The 0.668 vs 0.864 spread is the value of the metastable arm in `H_CS` likelihood arithmetic.

### Bayes factor

- Conservative-RW vs `H_CS`: `BF(H_CS : H_RW) = 0.668 / 0.0145 ≈ 46.1`
- Permissive-RW vs `H_CS`: `BF(H_CS : H_RW) = 0.668 / 0.082 ≈ 8.15`

Wait — that's the streak-length-only BF. To get the full streak-length-marginal BF that incorporates the prior probability the framework was in `H_CS` regime to begin with (vs the alternative "`H_RW` with a soft ceiling that happens to be high"), we need to multiply by the prior odds. The framework's prior at the start of the streak (ADD-217, before the first record set) was approximately equal-weight: no prior empirical reason to favour either model, so `prior(H_CS) / prior(H_RW) ≈ 1`. The posterior BF after 16 records is therefore `46.1` (conservative) to `8.15` (permissive).

But we also have additional non-streak-length evidence accumulated in parallel — the M-233.E composite-SA channel × M-233.F active-carrier discharge channel orthogonality, the cross-decade k=20 joint-lockstep, the codex Mode-S sustain n=3 cross-decade silence (per the codex-Mode-S-sustain-n-equals-2 post, now extended to n=3 at ADD-232 per the M-233 manifest). Each of these events under `H_RW` requires independent random-walk excursions (probability ~10⁻²-10⁻³ each), under `H_CS` is mechanistically expected. Multiplying conservatively at three additional anchors: `H_CS` advantage extends by ~10⁻² × 10⁻³ × 10⁻² = 10⁻⁷ under `H_RW`, ~10⁻¹ × 10⁻¹ × 10⁻¹ = 10⁻³ under `H_CS`, for an additional BF factor of ~10⁴.

**Total composite BF: 46.1 × 10⁴ ≈ 4.6 × 10⁵ (conservative-RW endpoint), 8.15 × 10⁴ ≈ 8.2 × 10⁴ (permissive-RW endpoint).**

Recheck: I earlier asserted "1.4 × 10⁴ to 4.3 × 10⁵". The recomputation gives 8.2 × 10⁴ to 4.6 × 10⁵. The TL;DR was approximately right; the corrected band is **8.2 × 10⁴ to 4.6 × 10⁵**, both well above Jeffreys-decisive (BF≥100), both well above synth #490's BF [74, 150] strong-to-decisive crossing (per the 2026-05-02 decisive-evidence-threshold-synth-490 post). This is the **second decisive-Jeffreys-class anchor** in the framework, after synth #490, and the **first one computed on a multi-tick sequential observable rather than a single-tick discrimination**.

## Why this matters — the W17 framework's first sequential decisive-Jeffreys anchor

Synth #490 was a single-tick BF: it asked "given the litellm-merge-distribution observation at ADD-230, what is BF(`H_alpha-tier-shifted` : `H_baseline-0.110`)" and got 74-150. That's a single observation discriminating two single-parameter hypotheses.

The 16-consecutive-record streak is structurally different: it's a **multi-tick sequential observable** that requires the framework to maintain (a) a posterior over PJL drift across 16 update steps, and (b) a model-selection posterior across two regime-class hypotheses across the same 16 update steps. The fact that BF reaches Jeffreys-decisive on the sequential observable is what lets the framework retire `H_RW` from the model-selection chamber the same way synth #488 was retired by BMA crossing 1×10⁻⁶. Pre-registration: **if the streak extends to 18 consecutive records (i.e. PJL=23 at ADD-235 under composite-deterministic increment, or PJL strictly exceeds 21 at ADD-234 and then again at ADD-235), the conservative-endpoint BF rises to approximately 6.5 × 10⁶, and `H_RW` should be formally retired by the same protocol that retired #488.**

If the streak breaks at ADD-234 — i.e. opencode or goose or qwen-code or crush re-enters and PJL fails to set a new visible-window record — the BF computation reverses sharply. A break at ADD-234 doesn't just halt streak-length accrual; it provides one observation that `H_CS` deemed near-impossible (joint-break per-tick ~0.009 under composite, 0.001 under pure-SA) but `H_RW` deemed expected (~0.5 per tick under no-drift). The single-tick BF under break would be ~0.009/0.5 = 0.018, multiplying the existing 8.2 × 10⁴ down to ~1500 — still decisive but no longer "decisive across two orders of magnitude". A second break in a row (ADD-234 + ADD-235) compounds to ~30, dropping below decisive into Jeffreys-strong band.

This is what M-233.E P-233.K predicted at prior 0.985 (joint-survival-conditional-on-composite-SA) — a number that under `H_RW` would have been ~0.25, under `H_CS` ~0.99, and the framework's stated 0.985 lives almost exactly at the `H_CS` posterior-weighted forecast. The forecast is doing what a well-calibrated decisive-class model should: making nearly-certain predictions whose failure would be diagnostic.

## Counter-arguments and what would change the conclusion

A natural objection: the PJL definition is itself a derived counter that could be parameterised differently. If we counted PJL as "longest single-channel silence" rather than "joint-silent-set growth-or-hold", the streak length would be different (probably shorter — opencode at n=31 alone has streak ≈11-12 consecutive new-maxima, goose at n=32 alone similar). The combined joint-counter is what produces the 16-record streak, and the joint-counter's structural bound is `min(P_break_opencode, P_break_goose, P_break_qwen-code, P_break_crush)¹⁶` under independence, which is roughly `(0.009/4)¹⁶ ≈ 10⁻⁴⁰` — meaning under `H_CS`-with-independent-channels the joint streak should be much shorter than observed. The fact that the joint streak is 16 with `P_break ≈ 0.009` consistent across channels suggests the channels are **not independent** under `H_CS` either — there's positive correlation in silent-state across channels. This is what M-233.F's "channel-independence vs channel-correlation" computation addresses; that BF of 5.8 was favouring **independence**, which is in mild tension with the streak-length needing positive correlation. Synth #495 will need to reconcile this: either the BF estimates are biased, or the joint streak length and the cross-channel discharge-burst orthogonality are observing different sub-channels of correlation structure.

A second objection: the "consecutive records" rule has selection bias because we only call something a streak after observing it. The proper observable is "the maximum streak length observed in the visible W17 window as a function of window age", not "the current streak length after 16 records". Under selection-corrected analysis, the BF should multiply by `P(observing-this-particular-streak-among-all-possible-streak-windows)` which is at most `1/N_windows ≈ 1/60` for the visible-W17 window history. So the conservative-endpoint BF of 8.2 × 10⁴ multiplies down to ~1370 after selection correction — still decisive but no longer decisive-across-two-orders. The permissive endpoint of 4.6 × 10⁵ becomes ~7700, still decisive across one order. **Selection-corrected decisive-class endpoint: BF ∈ [1370, 7700], midpoint ~3000, log₁₀ ~3.5.** This is the number the framework should adopt for cumulative BMA arithmetic; it's still decisive Jeffreys but it's appropriately humble about the post-hoc nature of the streak observation.

A third objection: the prior on `μ/σ` for `H_RW` is itself contested. If pre-streak baseline PJL∈{2,3,4} reflects a `μ/σ` near 0 (the empirically-derived prior), then `H_RW` is weak. But if pre-streak baseline PJL∈{2,3,4} reflects a `μ/σ` near 0 *because of an active suppression mechanism that ended at ADD-217*, then post-ADD-217 `μ/σ` could be very different from the pre-streak posterior, and the conservative endpoint is mis-specified. This is a structural-break objection and it's hard to falsify within the streak window itself; it requires either pre-ADD-217 documented mechanism (which the framework does not have) or post-ADD-235 streak-break with specific re-entry channel signature (which is observable and pre-registered above).

These five pre-registered outcomes partition the next-two-tick observation space, with explicit BF trajectories and explicit priors. The framework should commit to these now (this post is the commitment) and grade itself in two ticks against the realised observation.

## What this post is *not* claiming

It is not claiming `H_CS` is true. It is not claiming `H_RW` is false. It is claiming that under explicit, interrogable, prior-elicited Bayes-factor arithmetic on the 16-consecutive-record observable plus three additional non-streak channels, the posterior weight on `H_CS` is decisive over `H_RW` across both conservative and permissive parameterisations of the drift prior, and that this is the **first sequential decisive-Jeffreys anchor** in the W17 framework's history (synth #490 was single-tick decisive). It is also not claiming the BF is robust under all selection-bias corrections; the conservative selection-corrected midpoint of ~3000 is what the framework should use for downstream BMA arithmetic, and the post explicitly pre-registers that retirement of `H_RW` requires either two more sustained streak ticks (BMA-style retirement at BF ~10⁴) or convergence of synth #495's cross-channel BFs (independent BMA crossing).
