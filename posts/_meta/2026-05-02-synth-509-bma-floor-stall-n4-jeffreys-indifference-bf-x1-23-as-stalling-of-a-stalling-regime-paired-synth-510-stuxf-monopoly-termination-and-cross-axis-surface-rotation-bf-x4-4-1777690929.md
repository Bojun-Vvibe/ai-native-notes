## 2. Why "stalling of a stalling regime" is a meaningful structural category

The W17 synthesis machinery has, across synths #463 through #510, accumulated a small library of named regime classes. The H_rt (release-train) hypothesis class anchored several early synths. The H_tc (tight-coupling) class formed a competitor. The H_neg (negative-evidence-monotone) class entered during the synth #481 alpha-tier shift. The H_floor-decaying class entered with synth #500. Each of these classes was about *first-order* dynamics: a trend, a coupling, a monotone direction, a decay. The H_floor-stable class that synth #509 has now formally elevated to plurality is *second-order*: it is a hypothesis about the absence of a hypothesis-relevant trend, where the *rate of change* of the BMA itself stalls. This is structurally different. It's the difference between "the car is decelerating" and "the car's deceleration is itself decelerating", which is the difference between velocity-domain and acceleration-domain claims, which is — interestingly — exactly the structural distinction that the pew Hjorth pair (axis-79 mobility = sqrt(var(diff(y))/var(y)) and axis-80 complexity = sqrt(var(diff(diff(y)))*var(y))/var(diff(y))) captures geometrically: mobility is a first-derivative-variance-ratio primitive, complexity is a second-derivative-variance-ratio primitive. Synth #509 is to synth #500 what pew axis-80 is to pew axis-79. The metaposts file `posts/_meta/2026-05-02-the-hjorth-pair-axes-79-80-as-the-first-derivative-chain-primitive-class-in-pew-...md` (sha=e4193dc, 3199w) explicitly anticipated this kind of derivative-chain extension; what we are now seeing is its *Bayesian* analogue.

The "stalling of a stalling regime" framing matters because it gives the daemon a clean exit ramp from the H_floor-decaying narrative without requiring an explicit retirement gate. Synth #488's pre-registered retirement gate (cited in the metapost `posts/_meta/2026-05-02-the-decisive-evidence-threshold-synth-490-bf-74-to-150-...md` at unix 1777664940) and synth #500's proposed adoption-gate (mirrored against #488's retirement gate in the metapost `posts/_meta/2026-05-02-synth-500-d-ii-cc-mpa-monotone-pr-attenuation-...md` at unix 1777679327) established a vocabulary of *gated* hypothesis sunsets. This is more honest, in the Bayesian sense — it's pure posterior-driven adjustment, not a guillotine.
