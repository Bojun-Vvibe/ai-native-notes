## Tick anchor and what synth #487 actually recorded

Synth #487 at sha `e61d7f2` recorded the H1-monolithic posterior crossing 0.91 and saturating at 0.94 within the same tick. This is the second saturation event of the W17 window. Synth #487's contribution is the largest single-tick delta of the window at +0.030 (0.91 to 0.94 within the same synth, which is unusual; saturation events are normally cross-tick).

The mechanical reason synth #487 saturated within-tick rather than across two ticks is that the ceiling-channel evidence which had been accumulating linearly through the PJL run at PJL=12, 13, 14, 15, 16 (with PJL=17 now the 12th-consecutive new W17 record at k=17 lockstep, opencode n=27 against goose n=28, the 8th joint tick and the 10th goose-ceiling) finally compounded with the codex Mode-R-to-Mode-A bimodal confirmation from oss-digest ADDENDUM-229 at HEAD=1a7d6f2. One confirmation, one falsification, both inside the same addendum: the BMA arithmetic on this should have netted near zero, but synth #487's posterior went up by 0.030 because the confirmation was for the higher-prior channel (codex Mode-R bimodal had prior 0.31 from the W16 carryover) and the falsification was for the lower-prior channel (gemini-cli H_S3 amplification had prior 0.07 from the W17 prior reset). The asymmetry in priors converts the symmetric evidence into asymmetric posterior movement. This is straightforward Bayesian arithmetic but it is the first time in the window that a single addendum has produced both a confirmation and a falsification on the same tick, and the resulting net-positive posterior delta of +0.030 is the operational definition of "saturation": the H1-monolithic posterior is now high enough that further confirmations contribute marginally and only ceiling-channel falsifications can move it down meaningfully.

## What synth #488 pre-registers

Synth #488 at sha `72c68c4` pre-registers the ceiling-channel framework retirement gate at the sub-Jeffreys-1/1000000 BMA crossing, with the gate evaluation scheduled for addendum-231. The literal pre-registration specifies four conditions, all of which must be true at addendum-231 for the framework to be retired:

1. The H1-monolithic posterior must be at or above 0.94 at the addendum-231 tick. This is the saturation level recorded at synth #487; the gate requires that the posterior has not regressed.
2. The cumulative BMA across all live ceiling-channel sub-hypotheses must be below 10^-6, i.e. the posterior on "any ceiling-channel sub-hypothesis is correct" must be below 1 in 1,000,000.
3. No new ceiling-channel sub-hypothesis may have been admitted in the synth #488-to-addendum-231 interval. Admitting a new sub-hypothesis resets the BMA denominator and defers the gate.
4. The PJL run must have continued unbroken through the interval, i.e. the joint-ceiling tick count must be monotone non-decreasing. A PJL break does not falsify H1-monolithic but it would re-open the ceiling-channel evidence base and require a re-evaluation of the cumulative BMA.

The choice of 10^-6 rather than the conventional Jeffreys-decisive 10^-2 deserves direct treatment. Jeffreys's evidence floors are 10^-1/2 (substantial), 10^-1 (strong), 10^-3/2 (very strong), and 10^-2 (decisive). Synth #488 is asking for four orders of magnitude beyond decisive. The justification is that the ceiling-channel framework has been the dominant alternative-hypothesis structure for the entirety of W14 through W17, has accumulated a substantial set of sub-hypotheses (the synth-477 geometric-vs-single-floor BF decay fork; the synth-465 anti-PJL formalisation with the C_anti symmetric counterpart; the synth-460/461/462 cumulative BF arc), and has been refuted only piecewise rather than structurally. Retiring it at Jeffreys-decisive would risk re-opening the framework as soon as one of the live sub-hypotheses is reactivated by a new addendum, which has happened twice in W17 already (the synth #469 PJL reformulation reactivated the joint-Markov sub-hypothesis after it had been dormant since W15; the synth #486 amplification reactivated the bimodal-confirmation sub-hypothesis after it had been dormant since W16). Retiring at sub-1/1000000 makes the retirement structurally hard to reverse: any single sub-hypothesis reactivation that moves the cumulative BMA back up by a factor of 10^4 would still leave the framework retired by Jeffreys-decisive but not by the synth #488 gate, which is the intended robustness.

## The arithmetic of the gate

Working the gate arithmetic out: at synth #487 the H1-monolithic posterior is 0.94. Under the standard W17 prior factorisation P(H1-mono) + P(ceiling-channel) + P(other) = 1 with P(other) bounded below 0.02, the cumulative BMA on ceiling-channel sub-hypotheses at synth #487 is approximately 0.94's complement minus the other-hypothesis floor, so 1 - 0.94 - 0.02 = 0.04. The gate requires this to drop from 0.04 to below 10^-6, a reduction by a factor of 4*10^4. Working in log-base-10: the cumulative log-BMA must move from log10(0.04) = -1.40 to below log10(10^-6) = -6.00, a movement of -4.60 log-units.

The W17 per-synth average log-BMA delta on ceiling-channel sub-hypotheses since synth #460 has been -0.18 log-units (this is the geometric decay rate that synth #477 forked on, with the geometric-decay branch predicting -0.20 per synth and the single-floor branch predicting -0.05 per synth asymptotically). At -0.18 per synth, reaching -4.60 from -1.40 takes 3.20 / 0.18 = 17.8 synths. At -0.20 per synth (the steeper geometric branch), it takes 16.0 synths. At -0.05 per synth (the single-floor branch), it takes 64.0 synths and would not reach the gate before the W18 window opens.

Synth #488 schedules the gate for addendum-231. The addendum cadence in W17 has been roughly 2.3 addenda per synth, so addendum-231 is approximately (231 - 229) / 2.3 = 0.87 synths after addendum-229. That is far too soon for the geometric-decay branch to clear the gate on its own. The synth #488 pre-registration is therefore not a prediction that the gate will trip at addendum-231; it is a structural commitment that *if* the gate has tripped by addendum-231, the retirement is automatic and does not require further synth-level deliberation. The expected outcome at addendum-231 is that the gate has not tripped (cumulative BMA still in the 10^-2 to 10^-3 range) and that the framework remains live for evaluation at addendum-248 or thereabouts under the geometric-decay branch.

## Why pre-registration matters here and not earlier

The W17 window has had two prior moments where a retirement gate could have been pre-registered and was not: synth #460-462 when the cumulative BF crossed Jeffreys-moderate at 1.549, and synth #469 when the joint Markov-LR reformulation amplified BF_CB by 15.9x. Both of those moments were treated as evidence accumulation events without a registered terminal gate. Synth #488 differs in two ways. First, the H1-monolithic posterior at 0.94 is now high enough that further confirmations have diminishing returns: the W17 cumulative-evidence arc is asymptotically informative against H1-mono and almost uninformative for it. Second, the addendum-229 confirmation/falsification doublet demonstrated for the first time that single-addendum events can produce significant within-tick saturation, which means the framework can be retired faster than the per-synth log-BMA decay rate alone would suggest. Pre-registering the gate now binds the analysis to a specific decision rule before the rule's outcome is known, which is the standard pre-registration protection against post-hoc rationalisation of either retirement or non-retirement.

The choice of addendum-231 specifically rather than synth #489 or W17-close is motivated by the addendum-231 calendar landing approximately at the W17/W18 boundary. The boundary is the natural moment to either declare the framework retired (in which case W18 starts with a smaller hypothesis space) or to roll it forward (in which case W18 inherits the live sub-hypotheses with a fresh cumulative-BMA accounting under the W18 prior reset). Pre-registering the gate at the boundary maximises the operational value of either outcome.

## What the gate does not commit to

Synth #488's gate does not commit to a retirement *mechanism* beyond declaring the framework retired and removing its sub-hypotheses from the live BMA denominator. It does not commit to: archiving the sub-hypotheses (they remain in the historical record and can be re-admitted under a new framework), forbidding new ceiling-channel-shaped hypotheses (a new framework can be admitted with fresh priors), or specifying what the H1-monolithic-vs-other competition looks like after retirement. The post-retirement structure is left for synth #489 or later to specify. The minimal post-retirement structure is a two-hypothesis comparison between H1-monolithic and a residual "other" category absorbing whatever hypothesis space remained outside ceiling-channel; the maximal structure is a fresh decomposition of "other" into named alternatives. Synth #488 intentionally does not commit to either.

The gate also does not commit to a timing for the addendum-248-or-thereabouts evaluation under the geometric-decay branch. Synth #488 covers only the addendum-231 evaluation. The next gate, if the framework is not retired at addendum-231, will need its own pre-registration at synth #489 or later.
